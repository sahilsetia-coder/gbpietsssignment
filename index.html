3D Double Helix Engine

Real-time mathematically generated trigonometric visualization of ATGC base pairs.

Nucleotide Base Pairing (ATGC)

The engine now renders dynamic representations of Purine and Pyrimidine structures. Adenine (A) perfectly pairs with Thymine (T), and Guanine (G) pairs with Cytosine (C). Depth and scale are dynamically updated utilizing Z-axis pseudo-layering algorithms.
